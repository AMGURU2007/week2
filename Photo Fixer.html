Form Photo Fixer

Resize and compress application photos and signatures on your device. Files are processed in your browser.

1. Choose a form preset

The built-in values are demonstration values, not verified official requirements. Replace them using the current official application notices before submission.

2. Upload photo and signature

Tip: use a clear, front-facing photo. The crop guide is a visual aid; adjust the crop sliders and check the preview yourself.

3. Adjust photo crop

The dashed inner frame represents the target crop. This starter uses manual positioning rather than automatic face detection.

4. Generate files

Upload both images, choose the correct form requirements, then process.

Before you submit

  • Confirm photo and signature dimensions and file-size limits from the official form notice.
  • Check that the photo crop follows the form's face/head-position rules.
  • After downloading, check the actual file size in your computer's file properties.
  • Test with Wi-Fi turned off to confirm offline operation.
